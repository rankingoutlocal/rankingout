What exactly happens with my old leads?

You export them — old quotes, no-shows, past customers, anyone who enquired and went quiet. We load them into our system and run a conversation campaign designed to restart the conversation, not to blast them. Anyone who wants to book gets booked straight into your calendar. You show up. Nothing changes about how you run your business.

Won't that annoy my past customers?

It would if we blasted them. We don't. These are people who already raised a hand with you once, and the message is written to sound like a person who remembers them. Anyone who wants out is removed immediately and permanently.

Yes, and you keep it either way. It takes us about an hour. See the reason why above — we'd rather be straight about our motive than pretend it's charity.
